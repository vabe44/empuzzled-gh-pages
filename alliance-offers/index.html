Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Empires & Puzzles Alliance Offers

Q:What exactly is an Alliance Offer in Empires & Puzzles?

An Alliance Offer is a special in-game bundle. When you buy it, you get your purchased coins/items immediately, AND free gift boxes are automatically delivered to other members of your current alliance. In a full group buy (29 buyers), each buyer receives 28 bonus gift shares per quantity!

Q:How does the Group Buy make pulls so cheap?

If you buy an offer alone, you only get your own items + 0 gifts. But when 29 buyers organize on Discord, form a temporary alliance, and buy the offer together, YOU receive 28 free bonus shares per quantity from the other buyers! If the offer stock is x2, x3, or x4, you get 56, 84, or 112 extra shares! This drops the effective cost per pull from standard $1.00+ down to as low as $0.14 - $0.35 per pull.

Q:Is this 100% allowed by Small Giant Games / ToS?

Yes, 100%! Alliance Offers use Small Giant Games' intended in-game mechanic to reward alliance cooperation. Group buys are voluntary, safe, and fully ToS-compliant.

Q:Will temporarily leaving my alliance hurt Wars, Titans, or Events?

No! Zero loss to your home alliance. Wars are scheduled around war timers so you can use flags first (unused flags are lost if you leave mid-war, but war score and war chest stay 100% intact; during prep phase, return 10m before war starts). Titans keep your score and chest pauses briefly with zero progress lost. For Alliance Quests and Monster Island, simply complete stages, collect AQ chests, and spend MI energy before leaving. Mythic Titan scores and loot stay intact. Exchanges take under 30 minutes!

Q:Do I have to buy every alliance offer that comes out?

Not at all! Group buys are 100% voluntary and opt-in. Join any offer you want, and skip any you don't. Note: it is NOT free — once you sign up for an offer, you are strictly required to buy the exact quantity you signed up for (X1, MAX, etc.).

Q:What if a group is already full (29/29)?

Our custom bot features live Waitlists! If a group reaches 29/29, signing up places you on the Waitlist. You are automatically promoted if a spot opens before deployment, or sent a direct DM with a "Ready to Join" button if a spot opens after deployment. Multiple groups (Round A, B, C...) run for high-demand offers.

Q:How do you prevent scammers who join without buying?

We enforce a strict 0% tolerance Anti-Scam System and Trust Tier structure. Negative-Points (< 0) and first-time (0) members buy in Wave 1 under host supervision while Trusted members wait. Trusted members buy in Wave 2. Each completed exchange earns +1 Point (unlocking Trusted status, VIP perks, and milestone roles). Anyone failing to buy is permanently banned across all our platforms, publicly blacklisted, and tracked against ban evasion.

Q:How does the Alliance Offer Program 2.0 Discord system work?

Everything is automated via custom interactive panels in our Discord channel. Click the role panel for @Alliance Offers, turn Server DMs ON, click a group on the X1 or MAX panel, enter your IGN, and press "✅ Confirm Sign-up". The bot manages group channels, sends automated 30m reminders, provides 15m check-in attendance buttons, enables 1-click calendar sync with alarms (iOS, Android, Google, Outlook), and supports player spot swaps!
