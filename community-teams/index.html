Frequently Asked Questions

Q:How do I share my team to Community Teams?

In your personal Roster, go to the Teams tab and create or choose your lineup. Make sure the "Public Team" option is toggled on. It will immediately appear in this community discovery directory for everyone to explore.

Q:How does the hero combo filter work?

When you select one or more heroes in the filter bar, the database returns lineups featuring that hero combination. This makes it easy to discover how other top players position key tanks, synergize special skills, or build rainbow war defenses.

Q:Can I view the player's full collection and emblem paths?

Yes! Click the inspect icon on any team card to open the creator's complete public roster on Empuzzled. You can inspect their troop levels, talent node trees, master emblems, and backup squad compositions.

Q:What are the most common strategic placements for defense?

In Empires & Puzzles, teams are ordered Left Wing, Left Flank, Tank (Center), Right Flank, Right Wing. Tanks usually have high defense and health with game-changing specials, while fast snipers, dispellers, and revivers are positioned on flanks and wings to punish attackers.
