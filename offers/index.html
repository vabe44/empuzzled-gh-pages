Frequently Asked Questions

Q:How are Empires & Puzzles offers valued?

Offer valuations are based on community-established "fair value" benchmarks for in-game items (gems, tokens, ascension materials, etc.). These benchmarks are derived from the most efficient ways to acquire these items normally.

Q:Who maintains E&P Offers?

The site is created and maintained by mrchief, a well-known community member who also provides detailed infographics and game guides.

Q:What is the Goblin Balloon evaluator?

It's a specialized tool on enpoffers.com where you can select the specific chests you've found in your Goblin Balloon to see if the final price is actually a good deal compared to buying those items separately.
