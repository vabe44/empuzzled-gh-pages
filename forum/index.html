Frequently Asked Questions

Q:What happened to the official Empires and Puzzles forum?

The official Small Giant Games forum (forum.smallgiantgames.com) was permanently shut down. The community has since migrated to empiresandpuzzles.forum, a fan-built successor that preserves the spirit of E&P discussion.

Q:Where can I discuss Empires and Puzzles strategy and heroes?

You can join the community forum at empiresandpuzzles.forum for in-depth strategy discussions, hero reviews, and alliance recruitment. For real-time chat, the official and a fan-run Discord server is also available.

Q:Is there a Discord server for Empires and Puzzles?

Yes! There is an official Discord server as well as a fan-run server where players share tips, discuss new heroes, coordinate alliance recruitment, and get real-time advice on their rosters.

Q:How is Empuzzled related to the official Empires and Puzzles forum or E&P fan forum?

Empuzzled partners with empiresandpuzzles.forum — the fan forum provides community discussion while Empuzzled provides interactive tools like the Hero Database, Talent Simulator, Event Calendar, and Tier Lists.
