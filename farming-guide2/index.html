Frequently Asked Questions

Q:What is the best farming stage in Empires & Puzzles?

The best farming stage depends on what you need. For recruits, Season 1 stage 8-7 is the most popular choice. For experience per world energy, stages like 9-1 and 12-9 are efficient. For backpacks, stages 5-8 and 6-8 are recommended. Use the Empuzzled Farming Guide to compare all stages.

Q:How do I farm recruits efficiently in Empires & Puzzles?

Stage 8-7 in Season 1 is widely considered the best recruit farming stage, averaging about 8-10 recruits per run with only 3 world energy. Atlantis Rises and other special events can also boost recruit farming with reduced energy costs.

Q:What should I farm during Atlantis Rises?

During Atlantis Rises, farm Season 2 stages for reduced energy cost and bonus Atlantis coins. Focus on stages that give the resources you need most—recruits, backpacks, or experience. The reduced energy makes higher-cost stages more efficient.

Q:How do I find crafting materials for battle items?

Crafting materials like midnight roots, metal ores, and crude iron drop from specific province stages. Use the Farming Guide to find which stages drop each material. Higher provinces generally have better drop rates for rare crafting materials.
