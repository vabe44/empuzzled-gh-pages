Fated Summon Helper

Never leave your roster to luck again. Earn credits through standard summons and choose your next 5-star dragon or ascension material from a guaranteed rotating selection.

  • ✦Earn Credits The bar increases for every non-Legendary dragon summoned in standard portals (Ancient Dragon Summon, Legendary Dragon Summon, Silver Summon).
  • ✦Fill the Bar Reach 80/80 to claim a dragon. Summons stop counting once full until you've made your choice.
  • ✦Rotation The selection changes every 6 months. Your current counter always carries over to the next list.
  • ✦Strategic Pick Prioritize dragons with powerful assists skills to boost your team's performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Fated Summon event in Empires & Puzzles? ▾
Fated Summon is a recurring event where every summon you make earns credits toward a meter. Once the meter is full, you can pick one guaranteed Legendary (5★) dragon from a rotating pool. Credits carry over between events but expire after a set period.
How many summons do I need for a Fated Summon? ▾
You need to accumulate enough credits to fill the Fated Summon meter. Each summon type awards different credit amounts. Generally, it takes around 100–150 summons to fill the meter, though the exact number depends on the types of summons you perform.
Do Fated Summon credits carry over? ▾
Yes, Fated Summon credits carry over between events. However, they do expire after a certain period. Check the in-game timer to see when your credits will reset.
How do I choose the best dragon from the Fated Summon pool? ▾
Use the Empuzzled Dragon Database to compare dragons in the Fated Summon pool. Consider your roster gaps, the dragon Tier List rankings, and whether the dragon synergizes with your existing team composition. Focus on dragons that fill a role you lack rather than duplicating strengths.
