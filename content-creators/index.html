The Empires & Puzzles community has many incredible creators ranging from strategy experts to entertainment and summon video producers. Some of the most popular include McNaulty, Wiamz, LegoKostya, Peque, and Fizel. You can browse the complete list of featured community partners on this page and search by language to find videos in your native language!

Q:How do I get featured as a creator on Empuzzled?

We are always looking for passionate, high-quality content creators to highlight! If you consistently produce Empires & Puzzles videos, guides, or streams, you can reach out via our channel or jump into the community Discord to show us your work.

Q:Where can I find the latest strategy videos for new heroes?

The Community Feed section right here on the Content Creators page aggregates the newest uploads from all partnered creators in real-time. Whether it's a deep-dive review into a new hero, war footage, or tournament strategy, you'll find the freshest videos right at the top.

Q:Are there creators posting in languages other than English?

Absolutely! The Empires & Puzzles community is global. We feature creators producing content in English, French, German, Spanish, Russian, Polish, Ukrainian, and more. Use the language filter on this page to discover amazing creators in the language you prefer!
