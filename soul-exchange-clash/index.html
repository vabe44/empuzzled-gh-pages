Frequently Asked Questions

Q:Which hero is the best pick in Soul Exchange?

The best hero depends on your roster, but the Empuzzled community rankings show the real-time consensus on which heroes provide the most value across all cost tiers.

Q:How does the Soul Exchange Clash work?

Players are presented with head-to-head matchups between heroes currently available in the Soul Exchange. By voting for the better hero in each pair, a live Win Rate leaderboard is generated.

Q:Should I vote based on the 5, 15, or 30 soul tiers?

You can toggle between evaluating strict Tier-vs-Tier matchups (to see the strongest within exactly 15 souls) or purely Random Matchmaking to see if a 5 soul hero actually beats a 15 soul hero!
