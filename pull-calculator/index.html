How to read this page

Each pull is calculated independently. Ten pulls do not change the chance of the next pull.

“Chance after pulls” means the chance of seeing the target at least once, not the chance of receiving it on every pull.

Bonus draws are separate opportunities on each pull. They do not remove or replace the normal portal result.

Preset odds may change when the game updates. Use Custom mode if the in-game odds are newer than this page.
