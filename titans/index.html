Frequently Asked Questions

Q:How does the titan loot tier system work in Empires & Puzzles?

Your loot tier is determined by the titan star level plus a bonus based on your damage grade (A+, A, B, C, D). Higher tiers unlock more loot rolls, which increase your chances of receiving rare ascension materials. For example, an A+ grade on a 14-star titan gives loot tier 17.

Q:What loot tier do I need for rare ascension materials?

Rare ascension materials (unfarmable 4★ mats) can drop starting at loot tier 1, but the chances increase significantly at tier 9+ where you get additional ascension material rolls. Loot tier 14+ gives you the best odds with 3 ascension material rolls.

Q:Do rare titans give better loot in Empires & Puzzles?

Yes, rare titans provide a bonus loot roll that includes a chance at the rare titan-specific ascension material item. The bonus loot is in addition to regular titan loot, making rare titans especially valuable for progression.

Q:What is the best titan strategy for loot in Empires & Puzzles?

Focus on hitting the highest-star titans your alliance can defeat and aim for A+ or A grade damage. Use color-stacking against the titan element, bring mana potions and attack items, and coordinate with your alliance for maximum damage. Higher loot tiers from bigger titans dramatically improve your ascension material odds.
