Frequently Asked Questions

The Platinum Trader & Shop is a monthly recurring event. At the Platinum Trader, you can pick 1 free gift choice and trade in excess items from your inventory in exchange for Platinum currency. In the Platinum Shop, you spend that Platinum to buy exclusive and high-tier progression items such as Alpha Tomes of Limit Break, Legendary Tomes of XP, Alpha Aethers, Golden Emblems, and Summon Coins.
No! Any Platinum balance remaining on your account will never expire. It stays on your account until the next time the Platinum Trader visits. Note that your Platinum balance may not be displayed in your regular inventory bag between events, but it reappears as soon as the next Platinum Trader arrives.
Players must be at least Account Level 10 to access both the Platinum Trader and the Platinum Shop.
Every trade offer at the Platinum Trader and every item in the Platinum Shop has a stock limit of 1 per occurrence. You can perform as many different trades and purchases as you wish, but each specific trade line or shop item can only be claimed once per event.
At every event occurrence, the Platinum Trader provides a 100% free gift where you can pick 1 out of 3 item choices. No Platinum or inventory items are consumed when claiming this gift.
No. All trades at the Platinum Trader and all purchases in the Platinum Shop are final, just like other in-game marketplace transactions, and they appear immediately in your Recent Activity log.
The highest-value items are Alpha Tomes of Limit Break II (instant 2LB without consuming materials), Legendary Tomes of Experience (instant 85->90 level-up), and Tomes of Golden Emblems. If you have extra Platinum, Summon Coins and 4★ Trainer Troops/Heroes offer great secondary value.
