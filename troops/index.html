Empires & Puzzles Troops FAQ

What are the different troop types in Empires and Puzzles?

Empires and Puzzles features five troop rarities: Common (1★), Uncommon (2★), Rare (3★), Epic (4★), and Legendary (5★). Epic troops include four sub-types: Crit troops, Mana troops, Ninja troops, Magic troops, and Styx troops. Legendary troops have class-based synergies with primary and secondary hero classes.

How do Legendary troops work in Empires and Puzzles?

Legendary (5★) troops were introduced in Version 63. Each has a primary and secondary class, giving extra bonuses when paired with heroes of the same class. There are 10 different legendary troops per color (50 total). They can be summoned or converted from Epic troops in the Barracks.

What is the max troop level in Empires and Puzzles?

Max troop levels depend on rarity: Common (1★) max level 10, Uncommon (2★) max level 15, Rare (3★) max level 20, Epic (4★) max level 30, and Legendary (5★) max level 30. Troops are leveled in the Barracks.

Which Empires and Puzzles troops are best for mana speed?

Magic troops (Tower of Magic) offer the highest mana boost at 20%. Regular Mana troops start at +5% and reach +15% at level 29. Key mana breakpoints are: level 5 (+7%), level 11 (+9%), level 17 (+11%), level 23 (+13%), and level 29 (+15%). These breakpoints reduce the tiles needed to charge hero specials.
