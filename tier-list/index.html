Frequently Asked Questions

Q:What is the Empuzzled Tier List Maker?

The Empuzzled Tier List Maker is a free tool that lets you create, share, and remix Empires & Puzzles hero tier lists. Drag and drop heroes into custom tiers, add notes, and publish your rankings for the community to see.

Q:How do I create a tier list on Empuzzled?

Sign in, then drag heroes from the database into your tier rows (S, A, B, C, etc.). You can customize tier names and colors, add notes to each hero, and publish your list publicly or keep it private. You can also remix existing tier lists as a starting point.

Q:Can I share my Empires & Puzzles tier list?

Yes! Once published, your tier list gets a unique URL you can share on Reddit, Discord, YouTube, or anywhere. Public tier lists are also browseable by the community. You can even export your tier list as an image.
