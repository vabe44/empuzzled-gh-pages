Flutterfly's special skill is called "Corrosive Pollen". Deals 200% damage to the target and nearby enemies. The target and nearby enemies get -1800 defense for 4 turns. The target and nearby enemies receive 3040 Poison damage over 4 turns.

Flutterfly's assist skill is called "Cauterizing Blaze". Deals 350 damage to all enemies. Recovers 50 health for all allies. All enemies get -10% defense for 5 turns.
