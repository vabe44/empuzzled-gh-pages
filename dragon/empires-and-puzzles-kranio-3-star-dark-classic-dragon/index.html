❓ Frequently Asked Questions about Kranio

Q:What element and rarity is Kranio in Empires & Puzzles?

Kranio is a 3 star Dark dragon (Purple) with rare rarity.

Q:What is Kranio's special skill?

Kranio's special skill is called "Toxic Headbutt". All enemies receive 4000 Poison damage over 5 turns. All enemies get -30% mana generation for 5 turns.　 All enemies get -1500 defense against Special Skills for 5 turns.

Q:What is Kranio's assist skill?

Kranio's assist skill is called "Charring Blaze". Deals 350 damage to all enemies. Recovers 50 health for all allies. All enemies receive 100 Burn damage over 5 turns.

Q:What is the mana speed of Kranio?

Kranio has Slow mana speed.
