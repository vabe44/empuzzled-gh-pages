Frequently Asked Questions

Q:How do I find the best dragons in Empires & Puzzles?

Use the Empuzzled Dragon Database to filter dragons by element, rarity, spirit class, and mana speed. Browse our detailed dragon guides to find the best assist skills and special abilities for your team.

Q:What dragon rarities exist in Empires & Puzzles?

Dragons in Empires & Puzzles come in three main rarities: Rare (3★), Epic (4★), and Legendary (5★). Higher rarity dragons have stronger special skills and more powerful assist abilities.

Q:What are Assist Skills in the Dragon Database?

Assist Skills are unique abilities that dragons provide to your heroes during battle. Each dragon has a specific assist skill that can trigger when its elemental gems are matched, providing buffs or healing to your team.

Q:How do I get new dragons for my collection?

New dragons can be obtained through Dragon Summons, Dragon Dawn events, and seasonal portals. You can also earn dragon eggs and growth materials by progressing through the Dragon Outpost and participating in Dragon raids.
